MegaGem is a sealed-bid auction game of gems, loans and missions. The opponents here were tuned by genetic algorithms, and the real Python engine runs right in your browser. No server involved.

How a round works
  1. An auction card flips. Treasure (take 1–2 of the face-up gems), Loan (take coins now, repay at the end) or Invest (lock your bid, get it back plus a bonus).
  2. Everyone bids at once, in secret. Highest bid wins and pays. Ties go to the first tied player left of the last winner.
  3. The winner reveals a gem from their hand into the Value Display. That changes what every gem of that colour is worth.
  4. Missions pay out automatically to the first player whose collection meets them.
  5. The game ends when the auction deck or the gems run out. Hands are revealed, gems are valued on the chart, and the most coins wins.

Full rules: RULES.md

The first load downloads a ~10 MB WebAssembly Python runtime (Pyodide). After that it's cached, and every bid is computed by the same megagem package the GA tuners and tests use.
